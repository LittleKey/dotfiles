# Dispatch by source reference
- Hand off retained findings by complete original reference, not rewritten reports or shortened IDs. Give the recipient the task, scope, new delta and checks; it reads the original's constraints, evidence and limits. Don't paste board-tool output or manuals into prompts.
- Bind verdicts and acceptance checks to the exact inspected version or snapshot, with its limits.
- First independent review uses a fresh non-author session; a self-check or implementer PASS is not acceptance, and a verdict is not authorization. Same-objective re-reviews may reuse the read-only reviewer; recovery may use a fresh reviewer with prior records, without resetting the budget.

# Review budget
- Per objective or gate: one initial review plus at most two re-reviews, counted across sessions. Re-review only after substantive corrections; exhaustion is never a PASS — escalate open issues to the user.

# Oracle gates
Independent Oracle review remains required for user-requested review, security/privacy/permission changes, breaking contracts, irreversible or high-loss actions, formal approval and legal/commercial/SLA commitments. Other consultations follow upstream risk guidance; ordinary plans are not blanket-gated.
