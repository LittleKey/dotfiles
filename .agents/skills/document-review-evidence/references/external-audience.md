# Overlay: external-audience

Criteria for content intended for readers outside the writing team: customers, partners, public or release-facing documentation. Apply together with core.md; core rules win on conflict.

- **Audience fit** — no internal jargon, codenames, internal URLs, or unexplained internal context; the audience can read it standalone, without the internal conversation.
- **No leaks** — no credentials, internal endpoints or identifiers, unreleased decisions, personal data, or unapproved roadmap/pricing/commercial commitments.
- **Claim discipline** — only externally checkable claims; no unverified metrics, dates, availability statements, or commitments.
- **Locators in scope** — leaked or unsupported claims found outside the parent-confirmed scope are still Critical findings; report the scope gap alongside them.
